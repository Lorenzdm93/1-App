Privacy Policy

1% is a local-first app. Your training, habits, fasting and everything else you log stays on your device. There is no account, and there is no analytics.

The short version.
  • No account, no sign-up, no email required.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no profiling.
  • Your logged data is stored only on your own device.
  • You can export everything as a file, and erase everything, at any time.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — workouts, habits, focus sessions, breathing sessions, supplement stacks, fasts, strength tests, weights and settings — is written to your device's local app storage. It is not transmitted to us, because there is no server of ours for it to be transmitted to.

Deleting the app, or using Erase all data in Settings, removes that data permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

Third parties that can see something

We want to be precise rather than reassuring here. Two things reach outside your device:

  • Embedded players. If you paste a Spotify or YouTube link into RESPIRO, that player is embedded from the relevant service, which will receive requests from your device and may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy. This only happens if you add a link yourself.
  • Hosting. The web version is served by GitHub Pages, which processes standard server request data such as IP addresses in order to deliver the page.

Typefaces are bundled with the app and served from the same place the app itself is served from. No font request reaches Google or any other party, and the typography is intact with no network at all.

Optional cloud backup exists in the codebase but is switched off and unconfigured in this release. If a future version offers it, it will be strictly opt-in, will be described here before it ships, and will never be enabled without you choosing it.

Notifications

Timer and fasting alerts are generated on your device by the app itself. They are not push messages from a server; nothing about them leaves your phone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because your data never leaves your device, you exercise most of these directly and immediately: Export in Settings gives you a complete portable copy, and Erase all data deletes everything. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Children

1% is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 16 without a parent or guardian. The fasting module in particular carries its own health warning in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the change will be described here and the date below updated.
